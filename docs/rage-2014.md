# 2014 Barbarian Rage

Select the bundled `dnd5e.content.srd2014.feature.barbarian-rage` feature through
the public content workflow. On the Barbarian's combat turn, call
`combat_use_activity` with that activity ID and `declaration={}`. It spends one
bonus action and one source use. Level 20 uses the existing unlimited-resource
representation. Use `declaration={end:true}` to end voluntarily on the actor's
turn, spending a bonus action without another use.

Heavy armor does not prevent entering Rage. It suppresses the three benefits:
Strength check/save advantage, the class-scaled bonus on Strength melee weapon
damage, and resistance to bludgeoning/piercing/slashing. Current equipment is
checked at resolution; these defenses are not added to permanent traits. Finesse
attacks use the chosen ability. Ranged attacks and spell attacks do not receive
the damage bonus, and critical hits do not double it.

Casting and concentration are prohibited even in heavy armor. Activation ends
existing concentration and reconciles dependent effects, held Ready spells and
Witch Bolt links in the same transaction. Rejected casts spend no slot or dice.

The effect lasts ten canonical six-second ticks. Calendar relabeling cannot
extend it. Unconsciousness or death ends it immediately. At the end of the actor's
turn, it also ends unless the actor attacked a hostile creature or took positive
damage since their previous turn. Missing an attack still counts; objects and
nonhostile creatures do not. Temporary HP absorption counts as damage, immunity
and damage reduced to zero do not. Activity before Rage activation and reactions
between turns count. Turn settlement clears the activity record only after the
end condition is evaluated. The selected level-15 Persistent Rage feature removes
this inactivity end condition, but not unconsciousness, voluntary ending or expiry.

Finite uses recover on a completed long rest, never on a short rest. Activation,
use, action budget, effect, source receipts and concentration changes commit with
campaign/character CAS. The campaign binds the active effect to its exact grant
and clock; copied or reset effects cannot authorize settlement. Reconnect through
the returned actor slice and replay an unknown write with its original operation
ID. The local Host supplies protocol metadata.

This contract is edition-bound to 2014. Relentless Rage, Berserker Frenzy and other
separate class/subclass features are not implicitly implemented by this activity.
