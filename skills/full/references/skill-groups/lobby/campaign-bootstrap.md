# Campaign bootstrap

Choose the D&D system, 2014/2024 edition, locale, and advancement policy before
creating the campaign. Use the public bootstrap group only to list systems and
create that initial campaign.

After creation, discard the unbound exposure and open a campaign-bound exposure
with the returned `campaign_id`. Do not administer membership, rules, modules,
characters, branches, or snapshots through the unbound bootstrap surface.
